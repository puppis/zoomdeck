Fragments and steps

Reveal and hide elements on clicks, time the fades, advance automatically and skip events.

Open in the guide ↗

[Visible on entry, hidden at step 3]{#hint x=0.6 y=1.7 hide=3}

[Appears at step 1]{#one x=0.6 y=0.9 step=1 color=GREEN}

[Step 2 reveals this, then advances by itself]{#two x=0.6 y=0.1 step=2 color=YELLOW}
[[next: step=2]]

[Step 3 shows this and hides the hint]{#three x=0.6 y=-0.7 step=3}

[[show: #hint step=4]]
[[hide: #one step=4]]
[[skip_next: step=4]]

[Never shown: step 5 is skipped]{#five x=0.6 y=-1.5 step=5}

[Step 6 follows step 4 directly]{#six x=0.6 y=-2.3 step=6 color=RED}

Steps#

An omitted step (or step=0) makes an element part of the slide's initial content; step=N fades it in at click N and hide=M fades it out later. [[show: #id step=N]] and [[hide: #id step=N]] repeat changes as often as needed. Showing a visible element or hiding a hidden one is an error, reported with its line.

Numbers are local to each slide and need not be consecutive: a lone step=4 creates one click, not four. All changes sharing a number play together, followed by a pause; if a step also visits children, the parent's changes play first.

Timing#

Open in the guide ↗

Each fade lasts layout.animationTime unless the element sets run_time=S; lag=S delays its start inside the step, so one click can stagger several elements. A show/hide directive may carry its own run_time, lag and rate.

[Fades in over 1.5 s]{#slow x=-3.5 y=-0.4 step=1 run_time=1.5}
[Starts 0.5 s later]{#later below:#slow left-aligned:#slow step=1 lag=0.5}

Entrance and exit styles#

enter= and exit= on an element (or on show/hide directives) replace the default fades:

enter= exit=
fade (default), grow, write, create, draw fade (default), shrink, uncreate

write, create, draw and uncreate suit vector content (formulas and shapes).

Automatic advancement#

[[next: step=N]] advances to the following stop automatically once step N (and the child visits it triggers) has finished; step=0 marks the slide's entry. Chains of next play without clicks; backward and outline jumps show the recorded states without starting playback.

Skipping the next event#

[[skip_next: step=N]] omits exactly one following event: a fragment step, a child visit (with its whole subtree) or the return to the parent, even across numeric gaps. On a child's last step it suppresses the zoom back to the parent, which is also what skip_return=true on the include does more declaratively.

Animated images#

Images with play=once or play=loop play when they are revealed: finite ones finish before the pause, loops become a looping stop that the next click leaves. See Images.
