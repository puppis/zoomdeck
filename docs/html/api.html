The API, in full

Every export of the zoomdeck package, generated from the sources: deck definitions, the player, the slide runtime, the dialect's parser and renderer, the drawing helpers and the module contracts of a deck's own extensions.

This page is generated from src/index.ts on every documentation build: each export with its signature, its documentation comment, and the public members of classes and interfaces. It is the complete surface; the pages Embedding and Code-authored slides show the few calls most pages and decks need, and Extending a deck the shape of the modules a deck may add. Modules of a deck import from zoomdeck, and the engine as manim-web.
