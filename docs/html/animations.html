Animations

Move, rotate, scale, recolor and fade elements, play named effects, morph one element into another, follow paths, make geometry tremble, and move child viewports.

Open in the guide ↗

[[animate: #ball, step=1, x=6.5, run_time=1.2, rate=ease_in_out]]
[[animate: #card, step=2, angle=0.4, scale=1.3, color=ORANGE, opacity=0.6]]
[[effect: indicate #before, step=3, color=GOLD_E]]
[[transform: #before -> #after, step=4, run_time=1]]
[[move_along: #ball, step=5, path=#orbit, run_time=2, rate=linear]]
[[animate: #satellite, step=6, x=7.5, y=-3.6, angle=-0.3, run_time=1.5]]
[[include: #satellite x=5.5 y=-3.6 width=4 step=7]]

Animation directives play at a numbered step together with the fades of that step. They share run_time, lag and rate; targets must be visible at that step, and a target may be changed only once per step.

animate#

[[animate: #id, step=N, …]] interpolates toward a target state:

Attribute Meaning
x, y move the element's anchor (anchor= picks another point)
at= move the anchor onto another element's anchor or a point, as in Positioning
dx, dy add an offset to the move
angle rotate by that many radians about the center
scale scale by that factor about the center
opacity, color final values

The ID may name an included slide: its viewport then moves, rotates or scales, and the whole subtree follows, as in the guide where a child is slid and tilted before it is visited.

effect#

[[effect: name #id, step=N, …]] plays an engine effect without changing visibility:

indicate, wiggle, circumscribe, flash, focus, grow, shrink, write, create, uncreate, draw, wave. color and scale tune the ones that take them.

transform#

[[transform: #a -> #b, step=N]] morphs a visible element into a hidden one, which becomes the visible element afterwards, so later hide/show directives address the new element. Give the target step=N for the transform's own step: the morph then reveals it instead of a fade. Works best between formulas and shapes.

move_along#

[[move_along: #id, step=N, path=#shape]] moves an element along a circle, arc, polyline or polygon; rotate=true turns it along the path.

tremble#

tremble= is an attribute of shapes, icons, connections, attachments (braces, boxes and blobs), formulas and groups rather than a directive: it makes the points of the element's vector geometry vibrate, a few times per second jumping to new nearby positions, with nearby points moving together so that outlines quiver like a line in an animated sketch. The geometry is exact again whenever the vibration stops.

Value Trembles
tremble=true from the element's appearance on
tremble=N from step N on
tremble=N-M from step N until step M, like step=N hide=M

tremble_amplitude (0.04 logical units by default) sets the largest displacement and tremble_rate (12 per second) how often the points jump. Several elements may tremble at once, and tremble pairs naturally with sketch=true.

A trembling stop behaves like play=loop: the step's fades and animations play once, the deck advances by itself into a looping clip, and the next click leaves the loop. The left key jumps over such loops instead of playing them backwards.

[[blob: #a #b, #focus, buff=0.4, color=RED, sketch=true, step=2, tremble=2-4]]
[[icon: server #api x=0 y=0 tremble=true tremble_amplitude=0.06]]

Rates#

rate= takes smooth (default), linear, rush_into, rush_from, there_and_back, ease_in, ease_out, ease_in_out.

Sequencing#

Changes of one step run in parallel; lag staggers them and run_time sets each duration. [[next: step=N]] chains steps without clicks. Group members cannot be animated individually; animate the group.

Note Every directive compiles to an engine animation played by the same machinery as the fades, so clips, snapshots, outline jumps, replay and reverse playback treat them alike.
