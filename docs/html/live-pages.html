Live pages

Embedded web pages shown while their slide is focused, with a poster for previews.

Open in the guide ↗

[[iframe: assets/embedded.html, #page, x=5 y=-1.2, width=8, height=4.5, step=2, poster=assets/page.png]]
[[iframe: https://example.org/demo, #demo, below:#heading, width=10, height=6, title="Demo"]]

An [[iframe: url, #id, …]] embeds a web page. On the canvas it is a placeholder: the poster= image scaled into the slide, or an outlined box labelled with the title (or the URL). The placeholder takes the usual placement attributes plus width and height, step/hide, color and .class.

The live page appears over the placeholder only once the camera has come to rest on the page's slide. In parent previews, during zooms in and out, and after the camera leaves, the placeholder stands in. Interaction inside the page is real: click outside it to return the keyboard to the deck.

  • URLs must be http(s) or relative to the Markdown file; pages that forbid framing stay blank.
  • The page is not drawn by the pen or spotlight overlays and is absent from exported screenshots.
  • A poster is the only way to show the page's look in previews, since browsers do not allow rasterising another page.

Links#

Plain Markdown links in prose, [label](https://…), are underlined on the canvas and clickable wherever they are visible; see Text.
