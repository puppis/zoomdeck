Shapes, connections and attachments

Rectangles, circles, dots, ellipses, polygons, arcs and polylines; arrows and lines; braces, boxes and blobs.

Open in the guide ↗

[[rectangle: #box x=-6 y=-2.4 width=3.4 height=1.8 corner_radius=0.3 color=BLUE fill_color=BLUE fill_opacity=0.15]]
[[circle: #disk right-of:#box buff=2.2 radius=0.9 color=GREEN stroke_width=4]]
[[dot: #p x=-8.5 y=-2.4 color=YELLOW]]
[[ellipse: #e x=-5.5 y=-2.4 width=3.4 height=1.6 color=TEAL fill_color=TEAL fill_opacity=0.2]]
[[polygon: #tri points="(-2.5,-3.2) (-0.5,-1.2) (0.5,-3.2)" color=ORANGE fill_color=ORANGE fill_opacity=0.3]]
[[arc: #a x=3.5 y=-2.8 radius=1.4 start_angle=0 angle=2.4 color=RED stroke_width=4]]
[[polyline: #zigzag points="(2,-4.2) (4,-2.3) (6,-3.8) (8,-2)" color=ORANGE stroke_width=3]]

Shapes#

Every shape needs an #id. All accept color (the layout color by default), stroke_width (default 2), fill_color and fill_opacity (0 by default; polylines have no fill), opacity, the placement attributes of Positioning, step, hide and focus_only.

Directive Required Optional
rectangle width, height corner_radius
ellipse width, height
circle radius
dot radius (0.08; filled by default)
polygon points (three or more)
arc radius, angle start_angle (radians; x, y are the arc's center)
polyline points (two or more)

Point-defined shapes (polygon, polyline, arc) keep their coordinates unless they are positioned; points is a quoted, space-separated list of (x,y) pairs. Shapes serve as positioning anchors and as connection endpoints. Every shape, connection and attachment also takes sketch=true, which draws it by hand with a hatched or solid fill. Ready-made symbols for diagrams (people, servers, databases, clouds, …) are icons, which behave like shapes.

Connections#

Open in the guide ↗

[[arrow: #box -> #disk, #edge, label="**labelled** $\to$", color=YELLOW]]
[[line: #disk -> (-1.2,-5), #drop, color=GRAY, stroke_width=2]]
[[arrow: (-8.8,-5) -> #box, #pointer, color=RED, step=1]]

An arrow or line goes from one endpoint to another: an element ID (attached at the facing bounding edge) or a literal (x,y) point. They take an optional #id, a label with inline styling and math (placed above the connection, sized by font_size, set in font), color, stroke_width, opacity, step, hide and timing. Connections are created after all other elements, so forward references work; they cannot anchor other elements and are not hidden implicitly when an endpoint disappears.

Attachments#

Open in the guide ↗

[[brace: #e, #be, side=bottom, label="an ellipse", color=GRAY]]
[[box: #tri, #bx, buff=0.2, corner_radius=0.1, color=GREEN, step=2]]

A brace is attached to one element, named first; it takes side (bottom by default, or left, right, top), buff, an inline label with font_size and font, color and stroke_width. A box (a surrounding rectangle) and a blob (a smooth curve at a fixed distance) enclose one or several elements, listed before the first comma; they are described in Boxes and blobs. All attachments accept step, hide, opacity and timing, may attach to a group or an icon, and, like connections, are created after layout. Shapes, icons, connections and attachments can also vibrate over a range of steps with tremble=.
