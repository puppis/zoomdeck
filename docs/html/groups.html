Groups

Several elements laid out in their own coordinates and handled as one element of the slide.

Open in the tour ↗

::: {.group #automaton x=2 y=-0.5 step=1}
[[circle: #q0 x=0 y=0 radius=0.6 color=BLUE]]
[$q_0$]{at=#q0.center anchor=center}
[[circle: #q1 x=3 y=0 radius=0.6 color=BLUE]]
[$q_1$]{at=#q1.center anchor=center}
[[arrow: #q0 -> #q1, #t, label="$a$"]]
:::

[[box: #automaton, #gbox, buff=0.3, color=GREEN, step=2]]

::: {.group #legend below:#automaton left-aligned:#automaton buff=0.9}
[[dot: #k1 x=0 y=0 color=BLUE]]
[a state]{at=#k1.right anchor=left-baseline dx=0.2}
:::

A ::: {.group #id …} container holds elements, shapes, connections, attachments and nested groups (no includes, subslides or directives).

Local coordinates#

Members are laid out in the group's own coordinates: flow starts at the local origin, explicit coordinates and at= are local, and members may only reference other members of the same group. Member IDs without an explicit #id are prefixed with the group ID (automaton.element-2).

One element of the slide#

The group is then a single element: x, y place its origin (the local (0,0)) unless anchor= names a bounding-box point; relative constraints, at=, dx/dy, width (the members' wrapping width), opacity, step, hide, focus_only and enter/exit apply to the whole. A group can be an at= or relative reference, an arrow endpoint, a brace or box target, and the target of an animation.

Members cannot carry step, hide, focus_only or play, and show/hide/animate directives cannot target them: animate the group.
