This page is generated from `src/index.ts` on every documentation build: each export with its
signature, its documentation comment, and the public members of classes and interfaces. It
is the complete surface; the pages [Embedding](embedding.html) and
[Code-authored slides](code-slides.html) show the few calls most pages and decks need, and
[Extending a deck](extending.html) the shape of the modules a deck may add. Modules of a deck
import from `zoomdown`, and the engine as `manim-web`.
